# Gold: research file

Every real problem and missed opportunity found for the topic "gold", with its source. Pillars and posts are taken from this list (see `CONSTITUTION.md`, "The Approach" and §6).

**How to read it**

- First pass built 2026-10-08 by three researchers who each opened the pages they cite. They read through a tool that returns extracts, so **re-open the page and check the exact words and figures before using any row in a post.**
- "Single complaint" means one customer's unverified account on a complaints site. It shows the problem exists. It is not enough to name a company in a post.
- "Post" is blank until a post covers the row.
- Not searched, because automated access is blocked: Reddit, Quora, Amazon reviews, Google Trends. Anything the author pastes from them gets added here.

**How the pillars were made**

For each problem row the question was: why did this happen to this person? Three human reasons account for almost every row. They were not chosen first; they are what was left after sorting. The author accepted them on 2026-10-08. Each row is filed under its main reason. Each pillar is written as a series: one main article, then an episode for each specific problem or fix inside it, in the order listed. Series 1 is in progress; finish it before starting series 2.
