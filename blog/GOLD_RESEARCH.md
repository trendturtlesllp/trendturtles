# Gold: research file

Every real problem and missed opportunity found for the topic "gold", with its source. Posts are chosen from this list (see `CONSTITUTION.md`, "The Approach" and §6).

**How to read it**

- First pass built 2026-10-08 by three researchers who each opened the pages they cite. They read through a tool that returns extracts, so **re-open the page and check the exact words and figures before using any row in a post.**
- "Single complaint" means one customer's unverified account on a complaints site. It shows the problem exists. It is not enough to name a company in a post.
- The **pillars below are a proposal** and are not final until the author confirms them. They were made by grouping rows that share a cause.
- "Post" is blank until a post covers the row.
- Not searched, because automated access is blocked: Reddit, Quora, Amazon reviews, Google Trends. Anything the author pastes from them gets added here.
